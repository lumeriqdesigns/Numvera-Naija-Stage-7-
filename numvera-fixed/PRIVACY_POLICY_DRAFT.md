# Numvera Naija Privacy Policy Draft

Numvera Naija helps people organise plans, tasks, people, money records, dates and files.

The production version may process account details, workspace information, uploaded files, task activity and notification preferences to provide the service.

Users should be able to request account deletion and removal of their stored content. Workspace access should be controlled through authenticated membership and role permissions.

Before publishing, replace this draft with a reviewed policy containing the legal business name, support email, data retention periods, subprocessors, deletion procedure and contact address.
